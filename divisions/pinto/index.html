Rules

Full division rules

PitchingPlayers pitch. After a walk, the batting team's coach finishes the at-bat.
Pitching limits2 consecutive innings per game, 4 innings per week
BuntingNot allowed
Stealing and leadoffsNot allowed
BatsMust carry a USA, USSSA or 1.15 BPF stamp
SpikesNo metal spikes
HelmetsRequired for batters and the on-deck batter. Face masks are optional.

Equipment

The uniform is supplied: a White Sox jersey and hat in the team color.

  • Mitt
  • Bat, T-Ball through Mustang: 2¼" barrel
  • Bat, Bronco and up: 2⅝" barrel, USA approved
  • Batting helmet
  • Baseball spikes (non-metal until Pony)
  • Athletic supporter / cup
  • Sliding shorts, Pinto and up
  • Equipment bag
