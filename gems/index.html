The program

The Oak Lawn Gems offer softball for players who want to play at the next level beyond house league. Full-time teams play year-round.

Gems players are looking to continue playing in junior high, high school and beyond. Managers and coaches work together to develop the girls' softball skills and knowledge, and to teach a positive attitude and work ethic.

Tryouts are typically held in late July and early August. Teams are formed quickly afterward and practices begin in late August or early September.

In the fall, teams practice together and may play in a league, a tournament or round robin, or scrimmage games. The spring season begins in January with indoor practices, then outdoor practices and games once the weather breaks.

Most full-time teams play 7 to 10 tournaments in addition to pick-up games. Part-time teams play 3 to 5 tournaments in addition to pick-up games. All part-time players are also required to play in the house league. Travel softball players may only play for the Oak Lawn Gems during the season in which they are on a Gems team.
