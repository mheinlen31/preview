The League

To provide a safe, inclusive and positive environment where children learn teamwork, sportsmanship and dedication through baseball and softball. We are committed to fostering personal growth, building community spirit, and developing a lifelong love for the sport in all of our players, coaches and families.

FAQ

Do I have to be an Oak Lawn resident?
No. Anyone may join, regardless of where they live.
Are prices different for non-residents?
No. Prices are the same for everyone.
What payments are accepted?
Credit card, PayPal or Venmo, online at registration.
Do I have to buy a uniform?
No. Uniforms are supplied by the Chicago White Sox. Every house league team wears a White Sox jersey and hat, in a different color for each team. Players bring their own shoes and mitt.
What equipment does my child need?
A mitt, bat, helmet and cleats. Full lists for baseball and softball are on the Registration page.
When do practices and games start?
Practices begin in early April, weather permitting. Games begin in late April or early May and are played once or twice during the week and on weekends.
Is there fall ball?
Yes. Fall Ball runs in September and October. Fees are lower for players who played in the spring.
Are there travel teams?
Yes. The Oak Lawn Lightning (boys) and Oak Lawn Gems (girls) have part-time and full-time teams.
Is there a code of conduct?
Yes. It applies to everyone attending games. Read the code of conduct.
