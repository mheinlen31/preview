The program

The Oak Lawn Lightning program runs from August 1 through July 31. Tryouts are held in July and teams are formed by August 1. Teams have the opportunity to play, practice and work out all year.

The program was developed to prepare players to continue their baseball careers beyond 14U, into high school and college. The coaching staff works to develop players' skills and baseball IQ, and teaches them to be positive role models on and off the field.

In the fall, teams practice together and may play in a league, a tournament or round robin, or scrimmage games. The spring season begins in January with indoor practices, then outdoor practices and games once the weather breaks.

Most full-time teams play 6 to 10 tournaments in addition to pick-up games, and some also play in a league such as WSBL. Part-time teams play around 3 tournaments in addition to pick-up games. All part-time players are also required to play in the house league. Lightning players may only be rostered full-time with this club.
