Code of conduct

Anyone attending an Oak Lawn Baseball & Softball game is subject to this code of conduct.

We pledge to provide positive support and encouragement to all players, coaches and umpires. We will refrain from any abusive behavior such as insulting, belittling, negative cheering, or physically abusing others. We will show respect for the rules, our opponents, the officials, our teammates, and the tradition of the game.

Arguing an umpire's call in any form is subject to immediate ejection from the park. The game will not continue until the person has left the area.

All School District fields, the SAC facility and the Kolb complex are smoke-free.

Animals are not allowed at Kolb Field or the SAC facility.
