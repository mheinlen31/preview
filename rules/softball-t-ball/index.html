T­Ball Rules


T-Ball is an instructional league. Because many players have little or no prior knowledge of softball, coaches shall emphasize the basic fundamentals of the game. 

Based on feedback the league received after the 2025 season, the league has moved away from the former 20-date combined practice/game schedule based on the belief that the schedule felt overwhelming and that practices were being rushed. Accordingly, the league will instead use a 14-game schedule with the goal of having at least six dedicated practice dates during the season.  

Teams should still warm up prior to games but only to the degree necessary to prepare appropriately.  Practices should be used for dedicated work at fundamentals, including but not limited to:

  • Defensive positioning
  • Ready position before each play
  • Being ready to field a batted ball
  • Learning how to field a ground ball
  • Holding the ball properly
  • Throwing
  • Catching
  • Knowing which base to throw to
  • Holding the bat properly
  • How to stand in the batter’s box
  • Swinging the bat
  • Watching the ball
  • Base running
  • Knowing where to run after hitting the ball
  • Stopping at and staying on the base
  • Position identification
  • Listening to coaches’ instruction
  • Basic helmet and bat safety

Practices may take place at the T-Ball home fields (Sward and Williams Place) at any time when a game is not already scheduled. The T-Ball VP should establish a system for coordinating practice times. Suggestions include, but are not limited to, a shared Google Sheet listing available field dates and times that managers can review and reserve.

GAMES AT SAC MAY NOT BE CANCELLED OR FORFEITED WITHOUT AUTHORIZATION FROM THE LEAGUE

General Rules

  • Each team will have approximately 14 game dates.  
  • Rainouts generally will not be rescheduled by the league. Managers may elect to reschedule a cancelled game at the home field for their division, provided the field and time are available on an alternate date. Any rescheduled game should be coordinated with the league VPs.
  • Bases are to be 50 feet apart
  • Score will not be kept. All games end in a ‘tie’
  • Play should consist of 4 innings and have a one-hour time limit
  • Each team's lineup will be divided in half so that the first half of the lineup will bat in the 1st and 3rd innings and the second half of the lineup will bat in the 2nd and 4th innings
  • To help with learning the positions and keep the girls engaged, all positions should be rotated every inning
  • Every player must wear a helmet while batting and on the bases
  • All players are permitted on the field. Outfielders must be on the grass (Sward field only)
    • While all players may be on the field at once, a manager may choose to play only an infield (pitcher, catcher, 1st baseman, 2nd baseman, 3rd baseman, shortstop, and short center) and have the remaining players work with another coach in the outfield on fielding, throwing, hitting, or other fundamentals.
  • The temperature must be 50 degrees or above at the start of a practice or game. No play may occur if it is below this temperature
  • The temperature must be 90 degrees or below at the start of a practice or game. No play may occur if it is above this temperature
  • All players must wear their regulation Oak Lawn Girls’ Softball uniform consisting of a jersey, baseball pants (black shorts may be worn in hot weather), socks, and hat (if no defensive face mask is worn)
  • Even though this is a t­ball league, coach pitch will be part of the season – as a general framework:
    • The first 1/3 of the season, the girls will bat off the tee the entire game
    • The second 1/3 of the season, the girls will hit off coach pitch for the first two innings and off the tee for the last two innings
      • If the player is unable to hit a pitched ball after 6 pitches, the tee will be used
    • The last  1/3 of the season will be entirely coach pitch
      • If the player is unable to hit a pitched ball after 6 pitches, the tee will be used
  • HOWEVER, managers should discuss prior to games where they are in their season and how they would like to handle coach pitch versus tee.
  • A batted ball should travel at least 6 feet to be considered ‘fair’
  • Up to three coaches are allowed in the field to assist players with positioning and instruction
  • There are no lead­offs, base stealing, or an infield fly rule
  • Each hit is a single with the exception of the last batter in the inning who is a “home run hitter”
  • No batter is ever considered “out.” All players stay and run the bases regardless of out or safe
  • Runners cannot advance on overthrows
  • Coaches in the field shall make all umpiring calls
  • The home team is responsible for field set­up at least 15 minutes prior to game time
  • The visiting team is responsible for cleaning up the field after the game
